# Rekha Teacher User Guide

1. **Create a Whiteboard:** Click **New Whiteboard** from the dashboard.
2. **Teach & Draw:** Use the floating toolbar (repositionable to Left, Right, or Bottom) for Pen, Highlighter, Eraser, Shapes, Text, Sticky Notes, Equations (KaTeX), Graphs (JSXGraph), Images, and PDFs.
3. **Switch Backgrounds:** Click **Background** in the top bar to switch between Plain White, Blackboard, Green Chalkboard, Ruled Paper, Graph Grid, or Dotted Paper without losing content.
4. **Present:** Click **Present** for a distraction-free smartboard mode with Spotlight, Screen Curtain, Laser Pointer, and Class Timer.
5. **Live Classrooms:** Create a classroom, share the 6-character code with students, and click **Start Live Session**.
6. **Record Lessons:** Click **Record** in the top bar to capture your whiteboard canvas and microphone locally as WebM video.
